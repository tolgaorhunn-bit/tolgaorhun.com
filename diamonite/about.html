THE DIAMONITE STORY

Jewellery that begins with you.

Diamonite is a private, bespoke fine jewellery experience in Doha, built around the individuality of every gemstone and every person.

Our collection is an invitation to discover a stone that speaks to you. From there, the design journey takes place in person, through a thoughtful conversation about your style and your story.

We don't offer a catalogue of ready-made jewellery. Instead, we focus on considered, personal creations.
