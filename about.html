About

Twenty years of turning digital into a business channel

Currently leading Digital, CX and E-Commerce teams at Vodafone Qatar, focused on improving customer experience while turning digital channels into strategic business channels that drive growth, revenue and efficiency.

Path

Telecom, banking, telecom again — by design

The path runs from Turkcell and Türk Telekom, through product leadership on İstanbulkart's transit and payments ecosystem, into a full-stack digital transformation role in banking at Sohar International — and now back into telecom in Qatar, where digital, CX and commerce sit under one roof for the first time.

Each stop has centered on the same problem seen from a different industry: how to make a digital channel earn its keep — measured in active users, transaction volume, cost per acquisition, and revenue — rather than treated as a cost of doing business.

Reports to COO-level leadership at Vodafone Qatar, with responsibility spanning digital channel strategy, digital revenue monetization, channel operations, and UX across App, Web, USSD and IVR.
